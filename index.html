// 01 — the problem

  
Villages lose three things every single day

  
Water, waste and energy are connected problems. So we built one connected solution.

  

    

      
0M

      
Indians under high-to-extreme water stress

      
About 2 lakh people die every year due to inadequate access to safe water.

      
Source: NITI Aayog, Composite Water Management Index

    

    

      
0.3–0.4

      
million tonnes of rural solid waste per day

      
Most villages have no bins, no collection and no segregation, so waste gets dumped or burned.

      
Source: Ministry of Drinking Water & Sanitation estimate

    

    

      
0%

      
solar output lost to dust & pollution (up to)

      
Typical panels convert only ~15–20% of sunlight. In parts of India, dust and haze cut output by 17–25%.

      
Source: Duke University & IIT Gandhinagar study

// 02 — real-world applications

  
Where it actually works

  
Every module is low-cost, repairable and buildable locally, so it can scale from our tabletop to real places.

  

    
🚰
Panchayat water tank
Fair, measured water for every household from one community tank, with low-level alerts.

    
🏫
Village school
A BIN-BIN waste corner teaches segregation daily while the compost feeds the school garden.

    
🏥
Primary health centre
Self-cleaning, sun-tracking panels keep power steady for lights, fans and medicine fridges.

    
🏘️
Housing colonies & hostels
Shared tanks without quarrels: equal turns, live levels on everyone's phone.

    
🍲
Mid-day meal kitchens
Kitchen waste sorted at source turns into compost instead of smoke.

    
🌾
Small farms
Solar power for small pumps, and organic waste back to the soil as manure.

  







  
// 03 — the three modules

  
One village. Three brains.

  
Each module works on its own, so a problem in one never stops the others. Try the live demos below.







  

    
01
💧 Water ATM
Demand + availability based
ESP32
Blynk IoT

    
A large main tank feeds two smaller house tanks through two pumps. The system checks how much water is available, listens to each house's demand, and shares water in equal, measured turns, so nobody takes more just because they came first.

    

      📡 Ultrasonic sensor reads tank level→
      🔘 House raises a demand→
      🧠 ESP32 queues & alternates turns→
      ⛽ Pump delivers an equal dose→
      🚨 Low-water cut-off→
      📱 Live on Blynk

02
🗑️ BIN-BIN
ESP32-CAM vision
Conveyor belt
Servo flap

    
A conveyor belt carries trash to BIN-BIN. An ESP32-CAM looks at each item and decides: Biodegradable or Non-Biodegradable. A servo tilts the flap, and the item drops into the right compartment. Sorted at the source, wet waste can become compost and dry waste can be recycled.

    

      🏭 Conveyor carries trash→
      📷 ESP32-CAM captures item→
      🧠 Classifies bio / non-bio→
      ⚙️ Servo tilts flap→
      🗑️ Right compartment

03
🌻 PanelFlower
LDR sun tracking
Self-cleaning
Arduino Nano

    
Like a sunflower, PanelFlower turns to face the sun. Two LDRs compare the light on each side, and an MG90S servo tilts the panel towards the brighter side. Single-axis tracking like this can raise output by around 25–30%. A second servo sweeps a sponge wiper across the panel on a routine to clear dust.

    

      ☀️ 2 LDRs sense light→
      🧮 Nano compares L vs R→
      ↻ MG90S tilts panel→
      🧽 SG90 wiper cleans→
      ⚡ More power

// did you know? (tap to flip)

  

    
DID YOU KNOW?
Young sunflowers track the sun across the sky.
This is called heliotropism. PanelFlower borrows the same idea, but with LDRs and a servo instead of plant hormones. 🌻

    
DID YOU KNOW?
Mixed waste is the hardest waste to recycle.
Once wet and dry waste mix, the dry waste gets dirty and the wet waste can't be composted. Sorting at the source fixes both. 🗑️

    
DID YOU KNOW?
Our Water ATM's brain has built-in Wi-Fi.
The ESP32 has Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on board, which is how it talks to the Blynk app without any extra modules. 📶

// 06 — UN sustainable development goals

  
Connected to 9 SDGs

  

    
3
Good Health & Well-being
Safer water and less open burning of waste mean fewer illnesses.

    
5
Gender Equality
Reliable water at home cuts the hours women and girls spend fetching it.

    
6
Clean Water & Sanitation
Fair, measured, monitored water distribution for every household.

    
7
Affordable & Clean Energy
Sun-tracking, self-cleaning panels squeeze more power from the same sun.

    
9
Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure
IoT and computer vision made cheap enough for village infrastructure.

    
10
Reduced Inequalities
Equal turns at the tank: first-come no longer means take-the-most.

    
11
Sustainable Cities & Communities
Smart rural development, bringing urban-level services to villages.

    
12
Responsible Consumption & Production
Segregation at source enables composting and recycling; the model itself reuses waste.

    
13
Climate Action
More solar output and less burnt waste mean lower emissions.

  

  
Why rural development? Most of India's people live in villages, but smart tech usually reaches cities first. Gram-Urbanisation is low-cost, repairable and locally buildable, so villages don't have to wait for cities to get smart.
